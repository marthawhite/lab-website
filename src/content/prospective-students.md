I will not respond to emails, only the form. If you don’t hear from me, it means I have decided not to proceed with your application. 

An MSc at UAlberta, and in my group, is thesis-based and similar to the start of a PhD. Our focus is producing high-quality research and publishing. It is a useful way to become more of an expert in research and better gauge if a full PhD is what you want. If you really want to go straight to a PhD, then you can first join the MSc program and can convert halfway through, if we decide that makes sense. All graduate students (MSc and PhD) accepted to our graduate program get guaranteed funding (for two years and four years respectively). 

For domestic students, especially those at the University of Alberta, the best path to graduate school is to consider doing an NSERC USRA. These typically require a GPA of 3.7 or above. If you are interested in applying for an NSERC USRA with my lab, please email in before January so we can plan for the application.

The most important criteria for acceptance into the lab is attitude and general competence. I expect most graduate applicants to have a high undergraduate GPA (except for some universities where this is difficult to obtain). It is ok if you are not yet knowledgeable in reinforcement learning, but I do expect you to come in with programming skills, some machine learning background, some mathematical background, and ability to work and make decisions under uncertainty (which is what research is all about). My lab is also highly collaborative, so if you do not like working with others, then other labs might be a better fit.
