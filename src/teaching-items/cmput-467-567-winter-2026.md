This course is an advanced machine learning course, focused on deeply understanding the algorithms with a strong focus on understanding the math behind machine learning algorithms. 
	
This course follows CMPUT 267 Machine Learning I. This course is meant
    to provide more depth than 466, and relies on having knowledge
    from Machine Learning I. 
	
For graduate students, that may not have taken
    CMPUT 267, you can take CMPUT 566 as a precusor to CMPUT 567 or
    take CMPUT 567 as a standalone, assuming you have taken machine
    learning at the undergraduate level. 

See the website for the syllabus and a tentative schedule.
